BLANK DIRECTION
The intended fit is oversized by pattern, with a broad torso and dropped
shoulders. Try the Shaka Wear SHGDD above. Another local sourcing option is
the AS Colour Heavy Tee 5080: 8.2 oz/280 gsm, boxy relaxed fit and dropped
shoulders; the solid colors are 100% carded cotton. These heavier surfaces
must be tested with detailed DTG art; greater weight alone does not ensure
sharper printing.

ARTWORK AND PLACEMENT
Use exactly these six works:
1. Covenant
2. Snake
3. Adam enjoying his apple
4. Insanity (current color-graded v3 source)
5. Runnin'
6. Two Trees

Clean rectangular artwork stamps, centered on the front. Remove photographed
walls, outer unpainted margins, and frame edges. Preserve composition, color,
and brushwork. No added lettering, logos, slogans, or generative repainting.
Ecru is the concept color, not an approved supplier color or color match.
Portrait stamps retain portrait proportions; Two Trees retains its landscape
composition. Final print dimensions and placement must be checked on the
smallest offered garment. A 10–12 inch width is a sampling direction, not
an approved production specification.

PRINT METHOD
Sample DTG first for these full-color paintings and small initial quantities.
Ask for a soft finish, adequate cure, and no visible pretreatment stains.
Compare a process/simulated-process screen print only when quantities justify
separations for each distinct painting. Ask for physical examples of paintings
or photographic designs on the actual blank rather than judging logo samples.

These are concept assets. In particular, Adam, Snake, and Insanity need better
captures for crisp large prints. A 12-inch-wide print at 300 ppi requires
3600 actual pixels across after cropping. A 12 x 16 inch stamp requires
3600 x 4800 pixels. Capture larger to leave room for squaring and cropping.
Do not upscale these previews and describe the result as recovered detail.
Ask the printer to confirm the required color profile and final raster format.
The SVG files preserve existing JPEG data inside a cropped viewport; they
are not newly vectorized paintings and are not approved printer masters.

SAMPLE COMPARISON
Use Snake to check saturated greens/yellows and Covenant to check pale tones.
Compare the same source file, print size, placement, and blank between shops.
Check color in daylight, fine brushwork, ink feel, collar/fit, shrinkage,
pretreatment residue, and print change after several care-label washes.
Approve one physical sample of every design before selling the six designs.
Keep the approved sample and written production specifications for reorders.
