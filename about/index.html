About

I am an artist, engineer, and educator.

I have an MFA in Painting from the New York Studio School (2026), and degrees in computer science and philosophy from William & Mary (2017). I spent several years in sofware engineering, systems design, and digital infrastructure at various companies before I decided to pursue painting full-time.

I have taught and mentored at SEO, IMANI HOUSE, Environmental Leaders of Color, and Fairfax Collegiate. My teaching includes drawing, painting, mathematics, programming, and web development.

I offer custom art, lessons, studio visits, and web help. Get in touch.
